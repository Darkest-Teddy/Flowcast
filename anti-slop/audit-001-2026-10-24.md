# Map decluttering audit

Scope: the map's default presentation and its existing disclosure controls. User direction: audit first, then apply the cleanup. Keep the existing Flowcast tokens, campus models, activity colors and simulation behavior.

Design read: a campus exploration map with details on demand. ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1. The campus is the focal point; space around labels keeps buildings readable. Existing blue indicates interaction, while crowd colors retain their data meaning. No new decorative assets or animation.

## Findings and changes

1. **MEDIUM, R-31:** The desktop sidebar starts fully open, covering a large part of the map before a task is chosen. Start collapsed on both layouts, keep the weather chip and campus/day identity, and show live counts when expanded. Question deep links still open their answers.
2. **MEDIUM, R-31:** Building labels try to fill available space and stack upward. Limit ordinary labels by zoom and screen area; preserve names, prioritize interaction targets and events, and keep ordinary labels at their roofs. Layers offers the denser view explicitly.
3. **HIGH, R-32:** Clean view is keyboard-only and its return hint disappears. Add Layers → Map only and a persistent Show controls button; preserve H and restore focus to a visible control.
4. **MEDIUM, R-31:** Developer performance telemetry starts visible. Make it opt-in through the existing P shortcut or `?perf=1`.

The code review also found collision priority and hidden-menu focus restoration issues in the first implementation. Keyboard focus now outranks selection and hover, active labels may lift to avoid collisions, and focus restoration falls back to the Layers trigger if the initiating control is hidden.
